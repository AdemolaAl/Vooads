# Vooads sales playbook

For anyone who sells Vooads: founders, sales reps, partners and affiliates. Prices and credit costs come from `content/pricing.json`. If that file changes, this playbook follows it.

## 1. What we sell, in one breath

Vooads is an AI ad studio for people who run paid traffic. It makes UGC presenter videos, image ads in every Meta size, slideshows, hooks, scripts and copy in 20+ languages, and checks every script for wording that often gets ads rejected. The value is speed and volume of testing: more angles, hooks, presenters and markets per week, for less than the cost of a few creator videos.

Say it like this: "Vooads gives you fresh ad creatives every week without waiting on creators. Hooks, scripts, UGC videos and local versions, with a policy check on every script."

## 2. Ideal customer profiles

### 2.1 Performance media buyers (solo or small team)
- Run Meta, TikTok, Snap or Google for their own offers or as affiliates. Ecommerce, apps, lead gen, finance education, iGaming in licensed markets.
- Spend at least a few thousand dollars a month on ads, often across several geos.
- Pain: creative fatigue, slow and expensive creators, rejected ads, no budget for a video team.
- Buying trigger: a winner just died, they are opening a new geo, or an account got restricted after a risky ad.
- Best plan: Pro. Starter if they test one offer.

### 2.2 Agencies
- Run ads for several clients. Need many variations per client per month and reporting that shows testing velocity.
- Pain: production cost per client, slow turnaround, client approvals, margins.
- Buying trigger: new client onboarding, a client asking for "more creative", a creative strategist leaving.
- Best plan: Scale or Agency. More credits and longer videos. One login per account today: do not promise team seats, client workspaces or brand kits until they ship.

### 2.3 Ecommerce brands (DTC and marketplace sellers)
- In-house marketer or founder running ads. Product catalogue with several hero SKUs.
- Pain: real creator content is costly; they need product demos and UGC-style ads for many SKUs and for sale periods.
- Buying trigger: Q4, a new product launch, a new market.
- Best plan: Pro, then Scale in peak season.

### 2.4 App marketers
- Mobile app user acquisition teams and indie app founders.
- Pain: need many short creatives per week and many languages for app store markets.
- Buying trigger: entering a new country, a new feature launch, rising cost per install.
- Best plan: Pro or Scale.

### Who we do not sell to
- Anyone whose offer is a scam, a fake giveaway, a guaranteed-return scheme, impersonation of a real brand or person, or illegal where it runs.
- Gambling or financial operators who do not hold the licences and platform permissions their markets require.
If a prospect fits this list, end the conversation politely. Misuse leads to a ban without refund under our Terms. Say that plainly if asked.

### How to talk about price
1. **Anchor on the job.** "What do you pay today for one creator video, and how long does it take?" Let them say the number.
2. **Translate credits into ads.** "Pro is about 15 UGC videos of 15 seconds a month, or a mix: say 10 videos with scripts, 40 image sets, 10 five-second product clips and 400 hooks."
3. **Be precise about volume.** "50+ ad creatives a week" is true for a mix of videos, images, slideshows and hook packs on Pro. It is not 50 new UGC videos a week. Say so before they find out.
4. **Point at the cheapest test.** "Start with hooks on the trial. If the hooks are good, the scripts will be good."
5. **Default recommendation: Pro.** It has the AI script writer, which is where most of the time saving is.
6. **Discounts:** only use codes that exist in the admin. Never invent a discount on a call.

## 4. Discovery questions

Ask these before you demo. Listen more than you talk.

1. Which platforms and which geos are you running right now?
2. How many new creatives do you launch in a normal week? How many would you like to?
3. Where do your creatives come from today: creators, in-house, an agency, stock?
4. What does one video cost you, and how long from brief to file?
5. How long does a winner usually last before it fatigues?
6. Which verticals are you in? Any regulated ones: finance, health, gambling, crypto?
7. How often do you get ads rejected? What happened last time?
8. Do you need other languages? Which ones next?
9. Who else touches creative: a strategist, an editor, a client?
10. If this worked, what would you stop doing or stop paying for?
11. What would make you say no to a tool like this?

Write down their numbers. Use their words back to them in the demo.

## 5. Objection handling

**"AI videos look fake."**
"Some frames can look AI to a sharp eye, and it keeps getting better. The real test is your numbers. Run three AI UGC videos next to your current creatives in one ad set and compare hold rate and cost per result. If they lose, you have spent very little to find out."

**"My ads will get banned."**
"Ban Shield checks every script for income claims, health claims, personal attributes and other common triggers, and suggests safer lines. It lowers the risk. It cannot guarantee approval. No tool can. The platforms decide."

**"Platforms label AI content. Will that hurt performance?"**
"Platforms label realistic AI people, and you should use the disclosure setting where they ask for it. We do not have data that proves a label helps or hurts in your account. Test it."

**"It is too expensive."**
"Compare it with what you pay now for one creator video. Starter is $49 and covers about 4 UGC videos plus hooks and images. If the trial hooks are not useful, do not buy."

**"I already use another AI video tool."**
"Fair. What does it not do well for you? We are built for media buyers: hooks, scripts, videos, images and local versions in one place, a policy check on every script, and a refund on failed renders. Run the same brief in both and keep the better one."

**"I need real creators for trust."**
"Keep them for big brand moments. Use Vooads for the volume testing that tells you which angle deserves a real creator."

**"What if I do not use it enough?"**
"Plan credits reset monthly, so start on the smallest plan that fits your testing. If you use less than 10% in the first 7 days and it is not for you, you can get a refund."

**"Who owns the videos?"**
"On a paid plan you can use the outputs commercially, including in paid ads, with no watermark."

**"Can I use it for my trading or betting offer?"**
"If you hold the licences and platform permissions your markets require, yes, within the platforms' rules. The scripts will stay away from income claims and include risk or 18+ lines. If the offer is a guaranteed-return scheme or unlicensed, no, and the account would be banned without a refund."

## 6. DM scripts

Keep DMs short. One question per message. No links in the first message unless they ask.

### Telegram (media buyer groups, affiliate communities)
Message 1:
> Hi [name], saw your post in [group] about [topic]. Quick one: how are you handling creative fatigue on [platform] right now?

If they reply with a pain:
> Makes sense. We built Vooads for exactly that: hooks, scripts and AI UGC videos in 20+ languages, with a policy check on every script. Want a 2-minute look? I can send a sample in your vertical.

Follow-up (3 days, no reply):
> No worries if now is busy. If you ever want 20 free hooks for an offer you are running, the trial covers it. Just say the word.

### WhatsApp (warm leads, referrals, local agencies)
Message 1:
> Hi [name], [referrer] mentioned you run ads for [client type]. I work on Vooads, an AI ad studio for media buyers. Is creative production something you want to speed up this quarter?

If yes:
> Great. Easiest test: tell me one product and one market, and I will show you hooks and a sample script on a quick call. 15 minutes. What time works?

If they ask for price:
> Plans start at $49 a month. Most weekly testers pick Pro at $149, which includes the AI script writer. There is a free trial for hooks and images.

### X (public replies and DMs)
Public reply to a post about ad fatigue or creative costs:
> Same problem here. What fixed it for us was testing angles first, then hooks, then presenters. Happy to share the weekly routine if useful.

DM after they engage:
> Thanks for the reply. Here is the routine I mentioned: Monday 20 hooks, Tuesday 3 to 5 videos with 2 presenters, Wednesday image sets and slideshows, Thursday new hooks on the best video, Friday launch and cut. We built Vooads to run it. Want to try the hooks part free?

Rules for every channel:
- Never spam groups. Join, add value, then DM people who showed a real pain.
- Never claim results we cannot prove. No "our users 3x their ROAS".
- Never send screenshots of someone else's ad account or earnings.
- Stop after two follow-ups with no reply.

## 8. 15-minute demo script

Prepare: their product, their market, their language. Have the studio open on Create.

**Minute 0 to 2: confirm the pain.**
"Last time you said [their words]. Is that still the main problem?" Agree on one goal for the demo: "By the end you will see hooks, a script and a video for [product]."

**Minute 2 to 4: hooks.**
Create, Hooks. Type their product line live. Generate. Read three hooks out loud. Ask: "Which one sounds like your buyer?"

**Minute 4 to 7: script and Ban Shield.**
Switch to UGC video. Turn on the AI script. Show the hook, middle and CTA. Point at the Ban Shield line. If they are in a regulated vertical, paste one of their old risky lines into Ban Shield and show the safer rewrite.

**Minute 7 to 10: presenter and video.**
Pick a presenter that fits their market. Show the length setting and the cost. Play a finished sample of the same length. Label sample ads as AI-generated samples.

**Minute 10 to 12: scale.**
Show the language switch on the same angle. Show a slideshow and a hook pack as cheaper variations. Mention long videos are made in parts and stitched automatically.

**Minute 12 to 14: price.**
Map their weekly volume to a plan. "You said 10 new creatives a week. Pro covers that with room for scripts and images."

**Minute 14 to 15: next step.**
"Want to start on Pro today, or test the hooks on the trial first?" Agree a date to check in.

Never fake a live render. If a render takes minutes, play a pre-made sample and say so.

## 11. What we never promise

- Ad approval. Ban Shield lowers risk; platforms decide.
- Refunds outside the Refund Policy (/legal/refunds). Never promise a refund on a call or in a DM; point to the policy or hand it to support.
- Winning ads, ROAS, revenue, cost per result or any performance number.
- That AI presenters are indistinguishable from real people.
- Features that have not shipped: client workspaces, brand kits, priority queues not in the live plan list.
- Lengths, credits or prices that differ from the live pricing page.
- Results from "customers like you". We do not publish customer results we cannot verify.
- Help with offers that are scams, impersonation, fake giveaways, guaranteed returns or illegal in the target market. Misuse gets the account banned without a refund.
