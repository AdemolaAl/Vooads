# Short-form ad trend notes

Last updated: 3 October 2026 (week 40). Owner: Vooads admin. Update every Monday.

How to use this file:
- The AI brain reads it on every hooks, script and angles job. Keep it short and practical.
- Each note has a status: **Observed** (reported by several sources or seen in our own data), **Hypothesis** (worth testing, not proven), or **Retired** (stopped working; keep for one month, then delete).
- Write each note as an instruction the model can follow. Date every change.
- Third-party numbers below come from agency and vendor blogs. Treat them as directional. They are not Vooads data.

## 1. Platform and delivery

- **Observed (2026-10-03): creative diversity beats volume of near-copies on Meta.** Meta's ad retrieval system (often called Andromeda) is widely reported to group very similar ads and let them compete with each other. Variations should differ in angle, opening visual, presenter and structure. Copy tweaks on the same video count as one idea. Model instruction: in any pack, make each hook a different formula and label its angle.
- **Observed (2026-10-03): fatigue is faster.** Agencies report creative fatigue on Meta and TikTok in 1 to 3 weeks, down from 4 to 6. Model instruction: when a user asks for a refresh, change the hook and the first visual, keep the winning angle.
- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): 8 to 12 distinct concepts per campaign, 2 to 3 variations each.** Reported as a working target by Meta-focused agencies. Test it against your current structure before you adopt it.
- **Observed (2026-10-03): UGC-style creative leads cold traffic.** Creator-style, first-person video keeps outperforming polished brand film for prospecting in most agency reports. Keep polished assets for retargeting.

## 2. Length and structure

- **Observed (2026-10-03): 15 to 20 seconds is the default for most ecommerce.** Longer ads (30 to 60s) work for products that need explanation: SaaS, finance education, courses, high-ticket items.
- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): 21 to 34 second TikTok ads may lift conversions.** TikTok's own research is quoted for this range. Test a 25 to 30s cut of your winner against the 15s version.
- **Observed (2026-10-03): the first 3 seconds still decide everything.** Reported hook-rate targets: TikTok above 20%, Reels above 15%, Shorts above 12%. Use these as rough checks only.
- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): YouTube Shorts tolerates 45 to 60s explainers better than Reels.** Test long cuts on Shorts first.

## 3. Formats

- **Observed (2026-10-03): carousel and photo slideshow ads are growing on TikTok,** especially for ecommerce. Swipeable image sets with text and optional sound. Vooads slideshow output fits this. Model instruction: for slideshow jobs, write one idea per slide, 3 to 7 words per slide headline, and a hook on slide 1.
- **Observed (2026-10-03): photo slideshows of real product photos carry the lowest AI-label risk.** Platforms mostly label realistic synthetic people and scenes.
- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): text-led "listicle" slideshows (3 things, 5 mistakes) are cheap tests for new angles.** Use them to find angles before spending on UGC video.
- **Observed (2026-10-03): founder-led and talking-head native content works across Meta and TikTok.** For brands, a "why we built this" script with a clear demo stays strong.

## 4. Sound and captions

- **Observed (2026-10-03): sound matters on TikTok; captions matter everywhere.** TikTok reports most users rate sound as essential. Many Reels and Feed viewers watch muted. Model instruction: write spoken lines that read well as captions, and put the hook on screen in frame 1.
- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): a quieter, closer voice (ASMR-light, "telling a friend") holds better than a loud announcer read in beauty and home.** Test one calm-voice version per winner.

## 5. AI content and disclosure

- **Observed (2026-10-03): platforms label realistic AI people.** Meta uses an "AI info" label and reads C2PA metadata. TikTok has an AI-generated content toggle in Ads Manager and can auto-label, downrank or remove unlabelled realistic AI. YouTube asks for disclosure on content a viewer could mistake for real. Model instruction: never script the AI presenter as a named real customer or a real professional.
- **Observed (2026-10-03): EU AI Act transparency rules (Article 50) apply to synthetic content shown to EU users from August 2026.** Reported guidance: platform labels largely cover advertisers. Buyers running in the EU should switch on AI disclosure toggles.
- **Observed (2026-10-03): fake testimonials are treated as deception.** The US FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials covers AI-made fake customers. Model instruction: opinions are fine; invented customer histories are not.

## 6. Hooks that are working now (rotate monthly)

- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): "Things I wish I knew before..."** for education, finance education and first-time buyers.
- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): "3 things I check before I buy..."** for skincare, supplements, gadgets. Reads as helpful and keeps claims safe.
- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): "POV:" hooks** still pull on TikTok, may feel tired on Facebook Feed. Split test by placement.
- **Hypothesis (2026-10-03): price-anchor hooks against everyday spend** ("less than one takeaway coffee a week") for subscriptions and apps.
