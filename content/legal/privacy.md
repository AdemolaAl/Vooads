# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we ("Vooads", "we", "us"; the company named at the end of this policy) collect when you use Vooads at vooads.com (the "Service"), why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. We are the controller of this data, except for content we process on your instructions (section 5).

Vooads is used by people all over the world. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and, where they apply to you, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

Questions or requests: privacy@vooads.com.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account data.** Your email address, first name, country, brand details you add (brand name, niche, colour), marketing preference, referral code, and your Voo ID if you log in with VooSquare.

**Login and security data.** One-time login codes (stored only in scrambled form), login sessions (stored only in scrambled form), whether you chose "Keep me signed in", the browser and approximate IP address of each login, and records we use to stop abuse, such as how many accounts are created from one network.

**Billing data.** Plans and top-ups you buy, amounts, discount codes, payment method used, payment status and the payment reference or crypto transaction reference. Payment processors collect your card, bank or wallet details directly. We never receive your full card number.

**Content you add or create.** Product descriptions, scripts, prompts, product photos, logos, links to videos you ask us to dub or remix, and the Outputs we make for you (videos, images, voiceovers, music, captions, hooks and scripts).

**Support data.** Your support tickets and messages, call-back requests (including the phone number and call time you give), and notes our team adds to help you.

**Usage data.** The jobs you run, credits spent and refunded, errors, and when you last used the Service.

**Referral data.** Who referred you, who you referred, commission records and the wallet address you give for payouts.

**Email data.** Which service and marketing emails we sent you and whether sending worked.

## 2. Faces and voices

Some uploads and Outputs show faces or include voices. Under some laws this can be sensitive or biometric data.

- Only upload a person's face, likeness or voice if you have their written consent (see the [AI Content Policy](/legal/ai-content-policy)).
- We use these files only to make the Outputs you ask for.
- We do not use them to identify anyone and we do not build face or voice recognition profiles.
- Ask us at privacy@vooads.com to delete them at any time.

## 4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the providers that run parts of the Service, and only what each one needs.

### AI providers (they process your prompts, scripts, photos and audio to make your Outputs)

| Provider | What it does |
|---|---|
| Anthropic (Claude) | Writes hooks, angles, scripts and scene plans |
| OpenAI-compatible model providers, if we switch them on | Backup text generation |
| fal.ai and the models it hosts (such as Kling, Seedream, Wan, Sync Lipsync) | Makes images, videos, talking presenters and lip-sync for dubbing |
| ElevenLabs | Makes voiceovers and AI background music |
| Pexels and Pixabay | Supply stock footage for Standard animated ads (we send search words, not personal data) |

We may change or add AI providers. The current list is always available at privacy@vooads.com. We choose providers that do not use API data to train their models by default where that option exists. Providers may keep inputs for a short time for abuse monitoring under their own terms.

They receive your email, the amount and a payment reference, and collect payment details directly from you.

### Other service providers

|---|---|

### VooSquare

If VooSquare login is on and you log in with your Voo ID, VooSquare tells us your Voo ID, email and name. If your account is linked to a Voo ID, we send VooSquare limited activity (for example that ads were made, and the amount and plan of your payments so VooSquare can show your spending and handle its referral programme) and it can read your summary (ads made, languages used, credits left) to show in your VooSquare dashboard.

If our support team works from the VooSquare support inbox, your tickets, messages and call-back details, plus your email, name, plan and Voo ID, are shared with VooSquare so the team can help you there.

### Others

We may share data with ad platforms, payment providers or authorities to stop fraud or abuse or when the law requires it; with professional advisers; and with a buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganised.

## 5. Content we process for you

When you upload brand assets or make ads, we process that content on your instructions to provide the Service. You are responsible for having the right to use it, including the personal data of anyone shown in it. We do not use your Inputs or Outputs to train our own AI models.

## 6. How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account data | While your account is open. After you ask us to close it, we delete or anonymise it within 90 days, except what we must keep (below) |
| Login codes | Expire after 10 minutes; deleted within 2 days |
| Login sessions | 30 days from last use with "Keep me signed in", otherwise 12 hours from last use; deleted when they expire or when you log out |
| Uploads and Outputs | While your account is open, or until you ask us to delete them |
| Support tickets | While your account is open, then up to 2 years to handle disputes |
| Billing records | As long as tax and accounting law requires (usually 6 years) |
| Records of banned accounts | As long as needed to stop repeat abuse and handle claims |
| Marketing choice | Until you change it; if you unsubscribe we keep a record so we do not email you again |

## 7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your personal data;
- correct data that is wrong (you can also change your name, country and brand in Settings);
- delete your data and close your account;
- give your data to you in a portable format;
- stop or limit certain uses, including marketing (use the link in any marketing email or the switch in Settings).

Under the GDPR or NDPA you can also object to processing based on legitimate interest, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to a data protection authority (in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission; in the EU or UK, your local authority).

Email privacy@vooads.com from your account email. We reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm it is you. We may keep some data where the law requires or allows it, such as invoices or records of banned accounts.

## 8. Cookies and browser storage

We use a small number of essential cookies (for example the login cookie that keeps you signed in) and browser storage for things like your referral code. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).

## 9. Security

We use HTTPS, encrypt API keys and payment secrets stored on our servers, store login codes and sessions only in scrambled form, use passwordless email-code login, limit staff access by role and log staff actions. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

## 10. Children

Vooads is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Do not upload images, videos or voices of minors. If you think a child has given us data, email privacy@vooads.com and we will delete it.

## 11. International transfers

Vooads is run from Nigeria, and our providers work in several countries, including the United States and the European Union. Your data may be processed outside your country. When we transfer data, we use the safeguards the law requires, such as contracts with our providers.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is material, we will tell you by email or in the studio before it takes effect.
