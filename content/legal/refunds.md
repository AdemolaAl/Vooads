# Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

We want you to pay for Vooads because it works for you. This policy explains when we give refunds. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. New plans

You can get a full refund of a plan payment if both of these are true:

- you ask within 7 days of paying; and
- you have used less than 10% of the credits that payment gave you.

This applies to a first plan payment and to an upgrade, once per account. For a yearly plan, the 10% is counted against the first month's credits. If we refund a plan, the plan ends and its remaining credits are removed.

## 2. Renewals

Card plans (Stripe or Bachs) renew automatically, and auto-renew is on by default. We email you at least 3 days before every renewal charge, and you can turn off auto-renew with one click in Credits & plan. Your plan then stays active until the end of the period you paid for and is not charged again. See section 6 of the [Terms](/legal/terms) for failed payments, the grace period and price changes.

Renewal payments are not refunded. If you forgot to cancel and have not used any credits from the new period, contact us within 48 hours of the renewal and we will refund it. A renewal at a new price is only charged after we have emailed you about the change at least 14 days ahead.

If a renewal charge fails, nothing is taken. If a failed charge was later taken in error, or you were charged twice for the same period, we refund the extra charge in full.

Plans paid with crypto, bank transfer or mobile money never renew on their own, so there is nothing to cancel.

## 3. Top-ups

- An unused top-up can be refunded within 7 days of purchase.
- Once you use credits from a top-up, it cannot be refunded.

## 4. Failed jobs

If a job fails, times out, or every AI provider for it fails, the credits go back to your balance automatically. You do not need to ask. If you think credits were not returned, email support@vooads.com with the job number.

**Try again.** For 7 days after a job fails, Try again starts it once more and reuses everything that was already made (the script, the voiceover, finished parts). You pay only for the parts that are made again, never twice for the same part.

**Long videos made in parts.** If one part keeps failing after our automatic retries, the video waits in "Needs attention" instead of failing, and every finished part is kept. You choose:

- **Try that part again**: only that part is made again;
- **Use a still shot for that part**: free, made from the presenter or product photo with that part's voice; or
- **Cancel and refund**: all the credits for that video come back, the same as a failed job.

If you do not choose within 7 days, we cancel it and return the credits. Finished parts are kept for 7 days, so Try again can still use them.

**A lower quality model.** If the model for the quality you paid for is down and a lower quality model finished the job, the price difference comes back to your balance as credits automatically. We never move your job to a more expensive quality without asking you.

We do not refund credits for jobs that finished but that you did not like. Check the quote, script and settings before you start a job.

## 5. Free trial credits and discount codes

The 60 free trial credits and discount codes have no cash value and cannot be refunded or exchanged.

## 5a. Unused credits

Plan credits end with the plan period and are not refunded or carried over. Top-up credits do not expire while your account is open; unused top-ups follow section 3. When you close your account, remaining credits end with it. If we stop offering Vooads for a reason other than your breach, we refund unused top-up credits pro rata.

## 6. Banned accounts: no refund

If we ban your account for breaking our [Terms](/legal/terms), [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) or [AI Content Policy](/legal/ai-content-policy), for example for scams, fraud, anything illegal, impersonation or using someone's face or voice without consent, all remaining credits are forfeited and **we do not refund any plan payment or top-up**.

## 7. Crypto payments

Approved refunds of crypto payments (through Gatevoo or NOWPayments) are paid in USDT or the coin you paid with, to a wallet address you confirm, for the US dollar value of your payment. Network fees are deducted. We cannot refund payments sent to the wrong address, on the wrong network or in the wrong coin.

## 8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before you open a chargeback or payment dispute. It is usually faster.

If you open a chargeback, we may pause your account while it is open and share the relevant records with the payment provider. If the chargeback is not justified, we may close the account and recover the amount and any dispute fees. Referral commission on charged-back payments is cancelled.

## 9. How to ask for a refund

Open a ticket in the studio (Help → New ticket, topic "Billing") or email support@vooads.com from your account email. Tell us the payment date, amount and reason.

We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method where possible. Card and local payment refunds usually take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank or provider.

A refund of a payment returns the full amount you paid, in the way you paid it. Each method's processing cost is built into its price and is never a separate line, so prices can differ a little between payment methods; a refund always returns what you were charged.

## 10. Your legal rights

This policy does not reduce any refund right you have under consumer protection law where you live.
