# Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Vooads helps media buyers make ads fast. Speed makes misuse fast too. This policy sets the rules for what you may make with Vooads and how you may use it. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

The rules cover your inputs, your Outputs (videos, images, voiceovers, music, scripts and copy), your landing pages and the ads you run with Vooads content. They apply even if an ad platform approves your ad.

**The one rule that matters most: no scams and nothing illegal.** Accounts used for scams, fraud or anything illegal are banned without warning, lose all their credits, and get no refund.

## 1. What you may not make or promote

### Illegal products and services
- Anything illegal where you sell it or where your audience lives.
- Drugs, unapproved medicines, weapons, explosives, counterfeit or stolen goods, fake documents.

### Scams and fraud
- Fake giveaways, prizes, lotteries or "you have won" messages.
- Advance-fee, recovery and fake job scams; pyramid and Ponzi schemes; "get rich quick" offers.
- Fake shops, products that do not exist, bait-and-switch offers.
- Fake urgency built on false facts, such as fake stock counts or fake countdowns.

### Income and investment promises
- Guaranteed returns or income, or "risk-free" trading, investing or betting.
- Claims that a bot, signal group, course or system will make money for the viewer.
- Income or balance screenshots presented as typical.
- Trading, forex, crypto or binary options ads that hide the risk of loss or break the financial promotion rules that apply to you.

### Fake testimonials and fake endorsements
- Reviews or testimonials presented as coming from real customers when they did not.
- AI presenters described or implied to be real customers, doctors, experts or officials.
- Fake press coverage, awards or "as seen on" logos.

UGC-style ads are allowed: a presenter may speak about a product in the first person as a scripted spokesperson. The ad must not claim or imply that the presenter is a real, verified customer.

### Impersonation, deepfakes, faces and voices
- Impersonating any real person, celebrity, influencer, official, journalist or brand.
- Deepfakes of real people.
- Using any real person's face, likeness or voice without their prior written consent, including in uploads for photo-to-video, dubbing or hook remixes.
- Making an AI voice sound like a specific real person, or cloning a voice, without that person's written consent.
- Sexual, defamatory or humiliating content about a real person.

### Music and other people's work
- Uploading or remixing videos, music or images you do not have the right to use. You must own, or have permission to use, all footage you upload for Change background or Auto-edit, including everyone who appears or speaks in it.
- Asking for AI music that copies a specific artist's song, voice or lyrics. Brand music prompts must describe a mood, genre and tempo, never imitate a real artist, band or song.

### Health
- Claims that a product cures, treats or prevents disease without approval from the relevant authority.
- Miracle weight loss, guaranteed results, fake doctors or fake clinical results, fake before-and-after images.

### Adult content, hate and harassment
- Nudity, sexual content or sexual services.
- Content that attacks or demeans people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or similar traits.
- Threats, bullying, doxxing, terrorism or glorifying violence.

### Minors
- Anything that sexualises or endangers minors. We report it to the authorities.
- Images, videos or voices of minors in your uploads.
- Gambling, alcohol, tobacco, vaping, adult or high-risk finance ads aimed at minors.

### Gambling and betting
- Gambling or betting ads where gambling is illegal, or where you or the operator lack the required licence.
- Ads that present gambling as a way to earn income or escape debt, or that hide age limits or required safer-gambling messages.

### Crypto and financial scams
- Fake tokens, rug pulls, pump-and-dump schemes, fake exchanges or wallets, "double your crypto" offers.
- Loans or credit with hidden fees or false terms.

### Malware and phishing
- Ads or landing pages that spread malware, phish for logins or show fake security alerts.

### Elections
- False information about voting, fake statements by candidates or officials, or political ads that break the law or platform rules.

### Getting around ad-platform rules
- Cloaking (showing reviewers something different from what users see).
- Using Vooads content to dodge a platform ban, or on ad accounts you have no right to use.
- Misspelling or disguising words to trick review systems.

Ban Shield exists to help you write compliant ads. Using it to find ways around platform policies is a violation.

## 2. Misusing the Service

You may not:

- create several accounts to collect free credits, discount codes or referral rewards;
- share, sell or rent your account;
- try to get around credit limits, watermarks, content filters, the profit guard or security;
- use stolen payment methods or file false chargebacks;
- attack, overload, probe or scrape the Service, or send abusive messages to our support team;
- use the Service to build a competing product.

## 3. Labelling AI content

Vooads presenters and voices are synthetic. Many ad platforms and some laws require AI-generated or altered content to be labelled.

- Use the platform's AI or "synthetic media" label where its rules require it.
- Add an on-screen disclosure where the law that applies to you requires it.
- Never tell viewers an AI presenter is a real person.

See the [AI Content Policy](/legal/ai-content-policy).

## 4. How we enforce this policy

We use automated checks (including Ban Shield and our providers' safety filters) and human review. Depending on how serious the problem is, we may:

1. **Block** a job or download and tell you why.
2. **Warn** you by email and remove content.
3. **Restrict** features or pause your account while we review it.
4. **Suspend** your account for a period.
5. **Ban** your account permanently.

**Immediate ban, no refund.** We skip straight to a permanent ban for scams, fraud, anything illegal, impersonation, deepfakes or using a face or voice without consent, content involving minors, malware or phishing, payment fraud and cloaking. A banned account loses all remaining credits, its auto-renewal is cancelled, and no plan payment or top-up is refunded (see the [Refund Policy](/legal/refunds)).

We may also remove content, report illegal activity to the authorities, and share relevant information with ad platforms or payment providers where the law allows.

## 5. Report misuse

If you see Vooads content used for a scam, an impersonation or a deepfake, email support@vooads.com with links and screenshots. For copyright claims, use the [Copyright Takedown Policy](/legal/dmca).

## 6. Appeals

If you think we got it wrong, email legal@vooads.com within 30 days of our decision with your account email and the reason. We will review it in good faith.

## 7. Changes

We update this policy as laws, platform rules and risks change, and tell you about material changes before they take effect.
