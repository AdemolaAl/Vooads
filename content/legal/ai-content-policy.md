# AI Content Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Vooads makes video, voice, music, images and text with AI. This policy explains how AI content works in Vooads and what you must do to use it responsibly. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and works with our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

## 1. What is made with AI

| Feature | What AI does | Providers we use today |
|---|---|---|
| Hooks, angles, scripts, scene plans | Writes the text, using our ad playbook for your niche | Anthropic (Claude) |
| UGC talking videos, green-screen, podcast clips | Animates a synthetic presenter to speak your script | fal.ai (Kling AI Avatar) |
| Product videos, photo to video, Ultra-realistic animated ads | Generates video scenes | fal.ai (Kling, Wan) |
| Images, slideshows, Standard animated ads | Generates images; Standard ads also use stock footage | fal.ai (Seedream), Pexels, Pixabay |

We may change providers to keep quality high and prices fair. The current list is in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 2. AI presenters and voices are synthetic

Stock presenters and stock voices are AI-made. They are not real people and have never used your product.

- You may use them as scripted spokespeople.
- You may not present them as real customers, experts, doctors or any real, named person.
- You may not give them credentials or personal stories that make viewers think they are real, such as "I'm a nurse" or "I made this in my first week".

Any resemblance to a real person is unintended. If you think a presenter or voice looks or sounds like you, email support@vooads.com and we will review it quickly.

### Street interviews, testimonials and podcast clips

These formats are AI dramatizations. The presenter, the passer-by, the "customer" and the podcast guest are all AI actors following a script. Never present them as real people, a real interview, a real review or a real customer story. Do not add invented names, ages, jobs, statistics or results. Vooads can burn a small "AI-generated" label into any video for free from the chat ("add the AI label"), and can do it on every edit automatically if you switch that on in Brand kit.

### Your own actors

You can create actors from a description, pick a face and give it a voice. Actors must be fictional. We refuse descriptions and names that point at a celebrity, a public figure or someone you know ("looks like…", "a lookalike of…"), and we remove such actors if we find them later. You can also make an actor from your own face: tick the consent box and record a short clip of you saying the consent line on camera. Our team checks the clip before the actor can be used.

## 3. Consent for faces and voices

You may upload or use a real person's face, image, video or voice (for example in photo-to-video, dubbing or a hook remix of an existing ad) only if:

- the person is 18 or older;
- you have their prior written consent for this specific use, including AI generation and use in paid ads; and
- you can show us that consent if we ask.

Consent to appear in one video does not cover AI generation, dubbing or voice cloning. Get consent that clearly covers AI-made content. You may never use the face or voice of a celebrity, public figure or influencer without a written licence from them or their representative.

If you cannot show consent when we ask, we will remove the content and may ban the account.

## 4. Music

- Library tracks are licensed for use inside ads you make with Vooads.
- AI music is generated for your ad. Do not ask for music that copies a specific song, artist, voice or lyrics. Brand music prompts that name or imitate a real artist are refused.
- Brand tracks (Brand music) are made for your brand. You may use them in your ads, including paid ads, and reuse them in any Vooads video for free. You may not sell or distribute the music on its own, or register it with a content ID system.
- You must own the rights to any footage you upload (Change background, Auto-edit) and have consent from everyone who appears or speaks in it. Uploads are private, used only to make your video, and deleted automatically after a few days.

## 5. Pronunciation

We apply a pronunciation list (for example "Vooads" is spoken "Voo-ads") to every voiceover. Always listen to your ad before you publish; AI voices can still mispronounce names, numbers and brands.

## 6. Labelling AI content

Ad platforms and laws in many places require labels on AI-made or altered content, especially when it shows realistic people. You must:

- switch on the platform's AI or synthetic media label where its rules require it;
- add an on-screen note, such as "AI-generated presenter", where the law requires it;
- add a note anyway if a viewer could be misled about who is speaking.

## 7. Watermarks

- Outputs made with free trial credits may carry a visible Vooads watermark.
- Paid plan Outputs do not carry a visible watermark.
- Some Outputs may contain invisible watermarks or content credentials added by our AI providers. Do not remove or tamper with them.

## 8. Safety checks

Your request can be blocked by:

- Ban Shield, which flags ad copy that is likely to break ad-platform rules;
- our own checks for prohibited content;
- our AI providers' safety filters.

If a job fails or is blocked after it starts, the credits go back to your balance automatically. Repeatedly rewording a blocked request to get around a filter is a violation of the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 9. Accuracy

AI makes mistakes. Before you publish, check that every claim is true and you can prove it, that prices and offers are right, that translations and captions are accurate, and that the ad follows the platform's rules for your niche.

## 10. Ownership

As set out in the Terms, you own your Outputs to the extent the law allows, subject to our AI providers' terms. Paid plans include commercial use. Trial Outputs are for testing only. We do not use your content to train our own AI models.

## 11. Reporting and enforcement

If Vooads content uses your likeness or voice, impersonates you or misleads people, email support@vooads.com. We act fast on these reports.

Deepfakes, impersonation and using a face or voice without consent lead to an immediate ban, the loss of all credits, and no refund.
