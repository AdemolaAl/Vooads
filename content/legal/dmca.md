# Copyright Takedown Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

We respect intellectual property. Vooads users may only upload content they have the right to use. This policy explains how to report copyright infringement and how to respond to a report. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It follows the notice-and-takedown approach of the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), and we apply it to reports from every country.

## 1. How to report infringement

If you believe content uploaded to or made with Vooads infringes your copyright, email legal@vooads.com with the subject "Copyright notice" and include:

1. Your full name, postal address, phone number and email.
2. A description of the copyrighted work (for example a video, image, song or script).
3. Where the infringing content is: a Vooads media link, an ad link, or a screenshot that shows it.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by you, your agent or the law.
5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you own the copyright or may act for the owner.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We may share your notice, including your name and contact details, with the user who uploaded the content. Notices missing these details may be delayed or rejected.

## 2. What we do

When we receive a complete notice, we:

- review it promptly;
- remove or disable access to the content where appropriate;
- tell the user and give them a copy of the notice.

We can only act on content stored in or made by Vooads. If the content is in an ad running on another platform, report it to that platform too.

## 3. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, or that you have the right to use it, email legal@vooads.com with the subject "Copyright counter-notice" and include:

1. Your full name, postal address, phone number and email.
2. What was removed and where it appeared.
3. A statement that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification.
4. A statement that you accept the jurisdiction of the courts of Lagos State, Nigeria (or, if you are in the United States, of the federal courts for your district), and will accept service from the person who sent the notice.
5. Your physical or electronic signature.

We send your counter-notice to the person who filed the notice. If they do not tell us within 10 business days that they have started legal action, we may restore the content.

## 4. Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe copyright. Closed accounts lose their credits and are not refunded.

## 5. False claims

Do not send false notices or counter-notices. You may be liable for damage a false claim causes, and we may ignore notices from anyone who abuses this process.

## 6. Trademarks, faces, voices and impersonation

This process is for copyright. For trademark misuse, impersonation, or use of your face or voice without consent, email support@vooads.com. We handle those under the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) and [AI Content Policy](/legal/ai-content-policy), usually faster than copyright notices.
