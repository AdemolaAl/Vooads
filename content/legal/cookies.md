# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains the cookies and browser storage Vooads uses on vooads.com, in the studio and in the admin. The company behind Vooads is named in our Privacy Policy.

**Short version:** we only use what the Service needs to work and to remember a few choices. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Browser storage (local storage and session storage) works in a similar way. We call them all "cookies" here.

## 2. Essential cookies

These keep you logged in and keep your account safe. The Service cannot work without them, so there is no switch to turn them off.

| Name | What it does | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `va_s` | Keeps you logged in to the studio | 30 days with "Keep me signed in" (restarts each time you use Vooads); otherwise until you close the browser |
| `va_a` | Keeps staff logged in to the admin | Same as above |
| `voo_state` | Protects the "Continue with VooSquare" login while it happens (signed) | 10 minutes |
| `voo_attr` | Remembers which VooSquare partner link brought you here, so VooSquare can credit it when you sign up with your Voo ID | Up to 60 days |
| `va_voolink` | Connects your Vooads account to your Voo ID when you press "Connect VooSquare" in Settings | 10 minutes |

All login cookies are HttpOnly (page scripts cannot read them), sent only over HTTPS on the live site, and set to SameSite=Lax (other sites cannot use them to act as you). The cookie holds a random ID. The matching login record on our server is stored in scrambled form and ends when it expires, when you log out, or when you use Settings → "Sign out of all devices".

## 3. Browser storage that remembers your choices

| Name | What it does |
|---|---|
| `va_ref` | Remembers the referral code from a link you clicked, so the right person gets credit if you sign up |
| `va_last_email`, `va_admin_email` | Fills in your email next time you log in on this device |
| `va_keep`, `va_admin_keep` | Remembers whether you ticked "Keep me signed in" |
| `va_new`, `va_sup`, `va_prompt`, `va_sells` | Remember whether you have seen the welcome guide and the latest support replies, the ad idea you typed on the website, and what you told us you sell |

This data stays in your browser. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser data for vooads.com.

## 4. Third parties

Our fonts are served from vooads.com itself. Some older pages (such as the admin) still load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which can see your IP address. When you pay, the payment page of the processor you choose (for example Stripe or Gatevoo) may set its own cookies under its own policy.

## 5. Your choices

- Untick "Keep me signed in" when you log in on a shared device, so you are logged out when the browser closes.
- Use Settings → "Sign out of all devices" to end every login at once.
- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, you cannot log in.

If we ever add analytics or marketing cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

## 6. Changes

We update this policy when the cookies we use change. The date at the top shows the current version.
