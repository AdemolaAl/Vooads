Questions, answered.

Are the people in the videos real?

No. Every actor is AI-generated, so there are no model releases or usage fees. Follow each ad platform's rules for AI content.

I'm not a video person. Can I still use it?

Yes. Pick your type of business, type what you sell, and press the button. Vooads writes the script, picks the shots, adds the voice, captions and music. You can change anything in plain words.

What can I make?

UGC talking videos, street-interview style, podcast clips, product videos, photo to video, slideshows with voiceover, animated ads, dubs with lip-sync, hook remixes, brand music, background swaps and auto-edits of your own footage. Plus hooks, angles, scripts and image sets.

How much does a video cost?

It depends on the style, length and quality level. A 15-second UGC video on the Standard level is 225 credits. You always see the exact credits before you make anything.

What if a video fails?

Your credits come back automatically. Open a ticket and a person on the team takes a look.

Can I run the ads commercially?

Yes. On any paid plan, on any platform, with no watermark.

Which languages work?

24, including Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Swahili and Filipino. Each script is written for the language, never translated word for word.

How do I pay?

By card, in crypto (USDT or BTC), or with local payment methods in many African countries. Plans renew monthly or yearly. Top-ups never expire.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Turn off auto-renew in Credits. Your plan runs to the end of the paid period. See the Refund Policy.

What can't I use Vooads for?

Anything illegal, scams, fake reviews, pretending to be a real person or brand, or misleading health and money claims. Read the Acceptable Use Policy.
